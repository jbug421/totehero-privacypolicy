Privacy Policy for Tote Hero

Effective Date: June 28, 2026

Tote Hero ("the App") is developed by Bug Solutions ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

Summary

Tote Hero is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not operate any servers that collect, store, or process your inventory data. We do not show ads, track your usage, or sell any information to third parties.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your tote and item names, quantities, notes, or photos
  • Your barcodes, QR codes, or NFC tag identifiers
  • Your location
  • Any account or profile information (the App does not require account creation or login)

All inventory data you create in the App is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device data storage framework. It is never uploaded to us or any server we control.

Permissions the App May Request

The App requests the following device permissions only to provide the features described, and only when you actively use those features:

  • Camera — used to scan barcodes/QR codes and to take photos of items you add to your inventory. Photos are saved locally to the App's data store on your device.
  • Photo Library — used only if you choose to select an existing photo for an item.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — used only if you tap the microphone button to dictate an item name. Audio is processed using Apple's Speech framework. Depending on your device and iOS settings, this processing may occur on-device or be sent to Apple's servers for transcription; we do not receive or store this audio ourselves. See Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles speech recognition data.
  • NFC — used only if you tap an NFC scan button to read a tag's identifier on a tote or item. Tag data is read locally and is never transmitted off your device.

You can review or revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device's Settings app under Settings → Tote Hero.

Third-Party Services

When you manually enter or scan a barcode for an item, the App may send that barcode number to the Open Food Facts open database to attempt to look up a product name. No other information about you or your device is sent with this request. Open Food Facts is an independent, third-party open-data project; their handling of this request is governed by their own privacy practices.

No other network requests are made by the App during normal use.

Sharing Your Data

The App lets you voluntarily export and share tote contents (including photos, notes, and barcodes) using Apple's standard share sheet — for example, via Messages, Mail, AirDrop, or other apps installed on your device. This sharing is initiated entirely by you, goes directly through the destination you choose, and does not pass through any server operated by us.

If someone shares a .storagescan file with you, importing it stores that data locally on your device in the same way as data you create yourself.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you control its retention. Deleting a tote or item within the App removes it immediately. Deleting the App from your device removes all of its locally stored data, including the on-device data store and any saved photos.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all. The App is suitable for use by anyone needing to track storage inventory.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App's features. Any changes will be posted at this same location with a revised effective date.
